Chiquibaby Privacy Policy

App Privacy Policy · Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the El Show de Chiquibaby app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects on iPhone and Android, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is operated by DEG Entertainment for the Chiquibaby show.

1. Using the app without an account

You can listen to every episode without creating an account. When you listen as a guest, we do not ask for your name or email.

2. Information you give us when you create an account

You only need an account to post in the comments. When you create one, we collect:

  • Your name or display name, shown next to your comments.
  • Your email address, used to sign you in, reset your password and contact you about your account.
  • A user ID, created automatically to connect your account to your comments.

You can sign up with Apple, Google or an email address and password. If you use Apple or Google, we receive only the name and email that service shares with us. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it.

3. What you post in the comments

When you comment, the app sends what you choose to post to our servers: text, photos, short videos and voice notes. Comments are public. Other users of the app can see them, along with your display name.

The app asks for permission to use the camera and microphone only when you choose to record a video or voice note. You can say no, or turn this off at any time in your phone’s settings. Photos and videos you pick from your gallery are chosen by you through the phone’s own picker. The app does not look through your photo library.

4. Reports and blocking

If you report a comment or block a user, we store that report or block so that our team can review it and so that content from people you block is hidden from you.

5. Information collected automatically

To run the app, show ads and fix problems, the app and its service providers collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone’s advertising ID and a push-notification token.
  • App activity, such as which episodes are played and how the app is used.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics, to find and fix problems.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your internet (IP) address, used for ads and analytics. The app does not use your phone’s GPS.

6. Ads

The app includes Google AdMob, Google’s advertising service. When ads are shown, Google may use your device’s advertising ID, approximate location and app activity to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your phone’s settings: on iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising; on Android, Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

7. Service providers we use

These providers process information only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, we send you alerts such as new episodes. You can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings.

9. How long we keep your information

We keep your account and comments for as long as your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your account details, profile and comments within 30 days. Reports may be kept longer where we need them to keep the app safe or meet legal requirements.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open More → Delete Account.
  • Without the app: fill in the account deletion request form.
  • By email: write to info@chiquibaby.com from the email address on your account.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your information or to correct it.

11. Security

Information is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We use industry-standard measures to protect stored information, but no system is completely secure.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email info@chiquibaby.com and we will delete it.

13. Users outside the United States

Our servers are in the United States. By using the app, you understand that your information will be processed there.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes, we will let you know in the app.

15. Contact us

Questions about this policy: info@chiquibaby.com
